
# Music

## Wiring
To wire a speaker to play music, you first need to connect it to an audio amplifier. These will allow you to play music from another speaker and not the piezo buzzer already on the circuit board

* Soldering
  * The audio amplifier doesn't have pins connected to it, so you will need to solder breakaway pins to be able to connect it to the circuit board.

## Playing your own music
To add music onto the circuit board, you need to download the music onto the circuit board. While connected to the circuit board, you can copy music into the same place the code will be. (Keep in mind the limited amount of space)
