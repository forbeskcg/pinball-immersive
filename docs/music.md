
# Music

## Wiring
To wire a speaker to play music, you first need to connect it to a ___ board. These will allow you to play music from another speaker and not the piezo buzzer already on the circuit board

* Soldering
* Connect the speaker board to circuit board using the servo pins
* connect the speaker to the board

## Playing your own music
To add music onto the circuit board, you need to download the music onto the circuit board. While connected to the circuit board, you can copy music into the same place the code will be. (Keep in mind the limited amount of space)
